Honest scope — read before using results commercially

Claim conditions follow Codex CAC/GL 23-1997 (NRVs: Ca 800 mg, Fe 14 mg, vitamin C 100 mg, vitamin D 5 µg), with the EU protein variation from Regulation (EC) No 1924/2006. Nutri-Score uses the 2023 updated algorithm (Santé publique France, Q&A v. 10 July 2023; Scientific Committee reports 2022 & Jan 2023) — general foods and beverages only; the special cheese and added-fats/nuts categories are not implemented. Health Star Rating uses the tables in the Guide for industry to the Health Star Rating Calculator (baseline/modifying/final-score tables as summarised from the official guide); all six categories are implemented. National implementations vary, some claims need additional conditions (e.g. trans-fat limits for saturated-fat-free in some markets), and plain water’s automatic ratings are handled by the checkbox. This tool screens a nutrient profile — it does not calculate a profile from a recipe, and final claim legality always needs regulatory review.
